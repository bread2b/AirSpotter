The railway station is a high-score false positive. Parallel tracks and elongated structures may resemble some airport features.
Partial airport views, small targets, and background changes may contribute to missed detections.
These are visual hypotheses, not verified explanations of what the model actually used.
Softmax scores are model outputs and are not guaranteed probabilities of correctness.
Keep all misclassified samples. Do not remove images or change labels merely because the model predicted them incorrectly.

中文备注：铁路站属于高分误报。相似结构、局部视角和尺度变化可能影响识别，但尚未验证模型实际关注的位置。不能因为模型判错就删除样本或修改标签。

8. External Image Experiment

An external satellite image showed a triangular runway layout surrounded by farmland and buildings.
The user reported that the original framing failed, while a tighter crop with less surrounding content was recognized successfully.
This changed the field of view rather than merely reducing the image's pixel dimensions.
Both versions are ultimately resized to 224 x 224. Cropping reduces background and increases the airport's relative size at the same time.
The observation suggests sensitivity to framing in this example. It does not isolate whether background or target scale is the main cause.
The exact scores for the original and cropped images have not yet been recorded.
One external example is insufficient to estimate overall external performance.

中文备注：裁掉周围多余边缘后能识别，说明这次预测对取景范围敏感。裁剪同时减少背景、放大机场占比，目前不能确定哪个因素更重要。两张图的具体分数还未记录。
